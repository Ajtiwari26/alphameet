What is DeployMate Meet?

Traditional video calls are passive communication pipes that create meeting fatigue. DeployMate Meet turns technical conversations into verified engineering deliverables.

01

The 3-Way Triad

[FOUNDER + CLIENT + EVA AI]
Unlike generic bots that silently record meetings, Eva is a first-class LiveKit participant. She answers questions about architecture, asks clarifying questions to clients, and ensures both parties agree on technical boundaries.

Dual-Language Translation

[HINDI / ENGLISH / MULTILINGUAL]
Eliminate international language barriers. The founder speaks in Hindi, international clients hear natural English voice streams, and bilingual phonetic subtitles render in real-time on screen.
